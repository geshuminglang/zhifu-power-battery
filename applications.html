Household energy storage systems

Household energy storage systems

5 kW, 10 kW and 15 kW lithium battery solutions designed as a direct, higher-cycle-life alternative to lead-acid household storage. Quiet, maintenance free and ready to pair with an inverter.

  • 5 kW / 10 kW / 15 kW system options
  • Drop-in replacement for lead-acid banks
  • Lower self-consumption (≤ 50 µA per pack)
  • Stackable capacity for longer autonomy
