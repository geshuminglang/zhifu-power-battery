About us

Manufacturing power batteries in Huizhou since 2005

Situated in the vibrant manufacturing hub of Huizhou, China, Guangdong Zhifu Technology Co., Ltd. shines as a beacon of innovation and excellence in crafting power batteries and energy storage solutions.

Our extensive years of dedicated manufacturing and pioneering R&D expertise allow us to specialise in high-performance lithium-ion batteries, meticulously engineered to meet the ever-evolving demands of the global market. With our technologies and an unwavering commitment to quality, we deliver energy solutions worldwide.
